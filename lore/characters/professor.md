# Professor

Status: Seed

“Professor” is a role and development identifier. Name, field, and institutional
affiliation are open.

## Role

- **One-line identity:** The figure who plays the player in an ordinary game
  after the tavern confrontation and gives them their first king piece.
- **Story function:** Required bridge from the reactive opening into the chess
  tutorial, king connection, and surface Champion journey.
- **Primary quest:** [Opening tavern challenge](../quests/opening-tavern-challenge.md)

## Established foundation

- The professor interacts with the player regardless of their choice in the
  tavern confrontation.
- They play a normal game of chess with the player.
- They give the player their first king piece afterward.

## Open questions

- What does the professor study or profess?
- Why are they in the starting village?
- Did they witness the tavern confrontation, and does their response change
  according to the player's behavior?
- Why do they choose to test and equip this particular stranger?
- Which king do they give, and can the player choose among several?
- What do they teach about resonance, Chess Power, resignation, and harm?
- What connection do they have to the tournament organization or the occult
  track?
- Why is the interaction unavoidable without making the professor feel like a
  mechanical tutorial device?

# Characters

- [Tavernkeeper](tavernkeeper.md) — bright, practical young woman running the
  starting village tavern while caring for her sick father
- [Tavernkeeper's father](tavernkeeper-father.md) — dying former tavernkeeper and
  elder whose last words oppose E4's acquisition
- [Tavern brute](tavern-brute.md) — powerful orc-like outsider who bullies a
  child and becomes an early enemy or source of power
- [Tavern boy](tavern-boy.md) — clever, timid child whose future in chess turns
  on the player's first choice
- [Professor](professor.md) — plays the first ordinary game and grants the
  player's first king piece
- [Hood](hood.md) — guarded forest challenger from the existing prototype;
  identity and wider role unresolved

Create a character page when a person has an individual want, a relationship
that matters, or continuity that must survive across scenes. Background extras
can remain on their location page until they develop beyond a local function.

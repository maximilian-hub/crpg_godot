# Hood

Status: Seed

## Existing material

Hood is a guarded stranger encountered beside weathered forest statues. Their
authored name is hidden from the player even though the dialogue and assets use
“Hood” as a stable development identifier.

In the current dialogue, Hood:

- Speaks tersely and tolerates silence without trying to make the player
  comfortable.
- Is knowledgeable or curious about a legend of a king and queen who abandoned
  their realm to live in the woods.
- Briefly imagines doing the same someday, suggesting dissatisfaction with
  their present life or obligations.
- Is reluctant to play but claims to be unable to refuse the player's
  insistence.
- Treats the player as dangerously ignorant and warns that the games may be
  lethal.
- Responds to victory, defeat, a draw, and rematch choices, but does not yet
  reveal a larger goal.

The rematch dialogue includes references to “peons” and “devilish charms.” These
may imply status, arrogance, compulsion, supernatural magnetism, or simply an
early voice experiment; they are not settled facts.

## Current characterization

- **Surface:** aloof, weary, easily annoyed, observant
- **Contradiction:** wants solitude but remains available to strangers and
  formal challenge
- **Voice:** economical, formal without being flowery, with frustration leaking
  through restraint
- **Dramatic question:** what obligation or desire prevents Hood from leaving?

## Open questions

- Is Hood a real name, title, disguise, or development placeholder?
- What is Hood's actual relationship to the statues and the abandoned rulers?

- What king does Hood use, and what does that choice reveal?
- Is Hood a companion, recurring rival, guide, antagonist, or optional mystery?

# Tavern boy

Status: Seed

“Tavern boy” is a development identifier. His name is open.

## Role

- **One-line identity:** A timid but perceptive human boy whose future in chess
  is transformed by what the player does during his public humiliation.
- **Age:** No older than twelve
- **Primary quest:** [Opening tavern challenge](../quests/opening-tavern-challenge.md)

## Established opening behavior

- He is visibly shaken while playing the tavern brute before a crowd.
- Despite his fear, he finds a clever move that disrupts the brute's strategy.
- The brute's enraged response makes him cry.
- No one in the room initially intervenes.

His clever move is important: he has ability and agency before the player acts.
The player influences whether that potential is cultivated or crushed; they do
not create it.

## Branches

### Player stands up

The player inspires him to pursue chess seriously. He eventually becomes a
great player and aids the player later in the game.

### Player ignores or joins

The experience traumatizes him, and he puts down his chess pieces forever.

## Thematic position

The boy's branch shows that a seemingly brief act can alter another person's
possible life. The player can accept immediate risk to preserve his agency and
potential, or allow his vulnerability to become the price of safety or access
to the brute's favor.

His eventual role should preserve him as a person rather than turn him into a
delayed reward for a benevolent choice. If inspired, the form of his aid should
express the player he independently became.

## Open questions

- What is his name, family, and relationship to the village?
- Why was he playing the brute, and could he meaningfully refuse?
- Does he already love chess, or was he trying to prove or obtain something?
- What made his disruptive move clever?
- What kind of player does he grow into if inspired?
- How much time passes before he aids the player?
- What does he think of the player after being defended but seeing them lose?
- If abandoned, can the player ever encounter him again, and is his path truly
  irreversible?
- How do the onlookers relate to him before and after the event?

# E4

Status: Working

## Core identity

E4 is a singular megacorporation with influence in the public Championship
system and connections to the deeper institutional conspiracy. The exact
meaning of its name, corporate history, leadership, and full range of business
divisions remain open.

The surface Champion track currently culminates in the E4 Grand Finals.

## Land Acquisition division

E4 has a land acquisition operation capable of buying roads, land, or rights of
way for major infrastructure.

Before the bridge crisis, the division offered to acquire the road and whatever
land or rights are needed to build an interstate through or near the
[starting village](../locations/starting-village.md). The tavernkeeper's father,
then an influential elder, was adamantly opposed. The offer did not proceed.

Once the village's bridge becomes dangerously difficult to repair, the old offer
returns. E4 can promise professional infrastructure, apparent long-term security,
and a large cash payment. These are real benefits, especially to frightened
residents who do not know whether the village can safely solve the problem
itself.

The exact transaction is not settled. It must eventually specify:

- What E4 buys or controls
- Whether the interstate passes through, replaces, or bypasses the village road
- Who receives the purchase money
- Who owns, maintains, and controls access afterward
- What happens to traveler traffic, the tavern, the canyon camp, and nearby land
- Whether residents must relocate or surrender particular rights

## Relationship to the village

E4 is interested primarily in the transportation corridor, not in the village's
small economy, culture, honey, plums, or quarry. Its influence can therefore be
impersonal rather than openly sinister: the community appears on a map as an
obstacle, route, acquisition cost, or future service stop.

This replaces the earlier assumption that E4 had taken no direct interest at
all. The village is still peripheral to E4's larger designs; its road is not.

## Hidden involvement

At the wider story level, E4 participates in the network of corruption and
collusion connected to the ancient necromancer organization. The people handling
an ordinary land purchase do not necessarily know anything about that hidden
order.

The village plot should not require exposing E4's occult involvement. The land
deal works first as a surface example of institutional power and incentive.

## Thematic function

E4 offers the village a way to stop being responsible for a dangerous shared
problem. In exchange, it asks the community to surrender some combination of
land, control, identity, and future choice.

The offer should remain defensible. If E4 is obviously lying about every benefit,
selling becomes a gullibility test rather than a serious answer to the
[core question](../themes/core.md).

## Open questions

- What does E4 stand for, publicly and historically?
- How is E4 connected to the tournament organization and Grand Finals?
- Who represents Land Acquisition in the village?
- How aggressive, honest, or constrained is that representative?
- What does the interstate actually do to the existing settlement and traffic?
- What personal benefit can E4 credibly offer the player for helping the sale?
- How can E4 favor advance the Champion track without trivializing early
  progression?

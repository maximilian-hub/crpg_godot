# Quests and questlines

- [Opening: tavern challenge](opening-tavern-challenge.md) — the first playable
  thematic choice, followed by the professor and the player's first king piece
- [Starting village: bridge decision](bridge-decision.md) — a damaged bridge,
  E4 acquisition offer, local leadership vacuum, and the father's final words

Create a quest page once it has all three:

- Someone who wants something
- Pressure preventing a trivial answer
- At least one outcome worth remembering

A major quest should also have a specific variation of a documented thematic
question and people who embody competing answers to it.

Use the [quest template](../templates/quest.md) for a single quest or a linked
questline. Dialogue files and scene triggers are implementation; the quest page
owns intent, branches, outcomes, and persistent state.

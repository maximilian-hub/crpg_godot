# Current focus

Status: Working

## Active target

Use the [big-to-small writing process](writing-framework.md) to derive the
[starting village](locations/starting-village.md), its first characters, and
its first conflict from the same [core theme](themes/core.md).

The high-level premise, primary thematic question, dual-track player journey,
and village identity are now established well enough to begin that derivation.

## Established foundation

- The primary question asks how to live a good life when institutions treat
  people as disposable pawns.
- Cultivation and domination form the first major spectrum of answers, without
  reducing characters to a single axis.
- The player can complete a warm tournament ascent on the surface or discover
  and engage with the darker occult plot beneath it.
- The village is a warm, modest, unusually worldly mesa community whose life is
  shaped by travelers, cliffside trades, and negotiated coexistence with a
  nearby former bandit camp.
- The tavernkeeper is the first seeded village character.
- The opening tavern challenge provides the first personal-scale test of the
  theme, with persistent branches for intervention, inaction, and complicity.
- King manifestation, emotional resonance, connection, resignation, and harmful
  defeat have a working lore foundation.
- The village-scale conflict centers on dangerous bridge repair versus an E4
  land acquisition and interstate offer, exposed by the father's leadership
  vacuum and final words.

## Decisions needed next

Answer these roughly in order:

1. Why is the player naturally present for the deliberation and father's death
   on every opening branch?
2. What credible early-game reward makes helping E4 or lying personally
   tempting?
3. What exactly would E4 buy, build, control, and change?
4. What does local repair require, and who would bear its danger and cost?
5. Which three to five people embody distinct answers without becoming simple
   ideological mouthpieces?
6. How do free-roam microquests change relationships, evidence, or feasible
   bridge solutions before the meeting?
7. How does chess expose or transform the conflict?
8. What can the village become because of the player's response?
9. What surface-track hook sends the player onward, and what optional clue
   rewards players attentive to the subterranean track?

The [opening tavern challenge](quests/opening-tavern-challenge.md) answers the
first interaction-scale thematic question. It does not replace the still-open
village-scale branch design.

PRESENTATION SIZING INTENT
==========================

Purpose
-------

This document records the chosen long-term sizing policy for the game's major
presentation contexts. It is design intent, not a claim that the complete policy
has already been implemented.

The project will use a hybrid sizing approach rather than forcing the overworld,
dialogue UI, menus, and chess battle to obey one universal scaling strategy.
Each context has different artistic and interaction requirements, and should use
the simplest policy that preserves those requirements.


Canonical UI Composition
------------------------

Dialogue, menus, HUD elements, result screens, and other authored interfaces
should be designed inside a canonical 16:9 safe composition. Important content
must remain inside that area on every platform.

The operating-system window or physical device screen does not itself need to
be forced to 16:9. The canonical area should be fitted uniformly and centered
inside the available safe display region. Space outside it may use letterboxing,
pillarboxing, a flat thematic color, decorative extensions, or nonessential
background imagery. It must not reveal interaction-critical content or change
authored UI relationships.

Nonuniform stretching should never be used. Fractional scaling is acceptable
for generated interface artwork and other UI elements even when it sacrifices
strict pixel-perfectness. World rendering may retain a different scaling policy.

Landscape orientation is the intended presentation for phones and tablets.
Device cutouts, rounded corners, system gesture areas, television overscan, and
platform UI-safe-area requirements must be respected when fitting the canonical
composition.


Dialogue and Menu UI
--------------------

The dialogue UI should remain a fixed composition rather than reflowing or
changing its proportions for arbitrary window shapes.

The current reference is a 320x180 logical 16:9 canvas containing a bottom-
centered 304x78 dialogue construction. The portrait panel, nameplate, text panel,
seams, and ornamentation should preserve their authored relationships. Wider or
taller displays should not make the dialogue box wider, narrower, or otherwise
recompose it.

This decision permits the standard dialogue construction to use one complete
304x78 frame artwork rather than requiring a fully modular nine-slice treatment.
The complete frame may be generated and painted at a larger resolution, then
reduced and cleaned for its final presentation. A modular derivative may still
be created later if a genuine variable-size use case appears, but it is not a
prerequisite for the standard dialogue box.

Dialogue and general UI may use fractional uniform scaling. Typography and
filtering should be evaluated separately so that the chosen scaling produces an
acceptable result, but the world is not required to abandon integer scaling in
order for UI to scale smoothly.


Overworld
---------

The overworld should retain its centered square presentation and its existing
integer-scaling behavior. This keeps world pixels stable and prevents arbitrary
window proportions from changing the visible gameplay composition.

The square overworld presentation may coexist inside the outer canonical 16:9
stage. Dialogue and other UI should be positioned in the canonical UI layer,
not made dependent on the overworld subviewport's integer scale.


Chess Battle
------------

The chess battle is the deliberate exception to a strictly fixed 16:9 content
rectangle. Its board should use as much of the device's safe display region as
possible because square size directly affects touchscreen accuracy, especially
with an angled and foreshortened board.

The battle environment may fill the complete safe screen. Critical HUD and
authored staging should still respect a central 16:9 safe composition, but the
board may expand into additional vertical or horizontal space available on
16:10, 4:3, ultrawide, or other aspect ratios.

Future battle UI will include approximately:

* A stone player console in the lower-left.
* A corresponding opponent console in the upper-right.
* Player and opponent portraits or identity presentation.
* Turn and cooldown indicators.
* Access to settings, class skills, emotes, surrender, offer-draw, and other
  future actions.

These consoles should preferably be compact horizontal corner panels rather
than tall permanent sidebars. Frequently needed status and tactical actions may
remain visible; infrequent actions should open a temporary drawer, modal, or
secondary menu rather than permanently consuming board space.

The player panel rectangle, opponent panel rectangle, device safe area, hands,
pieces, and any other required exclusion zones will become explicit constraints
in battle layout. The board should be the largest valid projection that does not
intersect those constraints. In conceptual terms:

    maximum board size = largest projection that fits within
                         the safe display region
                         without intersecting reserved UI regions

This avoids reserving generic sidebars and avoids automatically shrinking the
board to a strict letterboxed 16:9 rectangle. On a squarer tablet, the board may
shrink only by the amount actually required by the authored panels, rather than
losing all space outside a fitted 16:9 area.

The current projection already leaves useful bands above and below the playable
surface. The upper-right and lower-left placements should exploit those regions,
including the empty space around the trapezoidal board, while never covering
playable square polygons or important piece/hand presentation.


Mobile Interaction
------------------

Physical tablet size may compensate for some proportional loss of screen area,
but layout must not assume that resolution or aspect ratio reliably describes a
device's physical dimensions. Board sizing and touch interaction should still
be tested against practical minimum touch targets.

Future mobile battle interaction may additionally use touch-down highlighting,
touch-up confirmation, drag cancellation, strong selected-square feedback, or
other input assistance. These interaction improvements are complementary to,
not replacements for, maximizing board size.

If wide phones provide otherwise unused space outside the central composition,
future touch controls may occupy those safe outer regions without changing the
authored game view.


Planned Validation
------------------

Before battle panel artwork is finalized, create a Battle UI Layout Lab using
plain placeholder rectangles and the real angled board. It should exercise at
least 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, and ultrawide-phone display shapes and report projected
square dimensions.

The Lab should be used to determine:

* Final player and opponent console dimensions.
* Whether compact mobile variants are required.
* The exact board-clearance constraints.
* Minimum comfortable projected square sizes.
* Collisions with pieces, hands, dialogue, and device safe areas.
* Whether temporary menus should overlay, displace, or pause the board.

No battle sizing or panel implementation should be committed solely from the
provisional example dimensions discussed during planning. The constraint policy
is locked in; exact panel sizes remain an empirical layout decision.


Decision Summary
----------------

* Dialogue and authored UI: fixed canonical 16:9 composition.
* Standard dialogue construction: fixed 304x78 logical artwork is permitted.
* Overworld: centered square with integer scaling.
* Chess battle environment: fills the safe screen.
* Chessboard: fluid and maximized subject to explicit UI and safe-area
  constraints.
* Battle HUD: critical information remains safe; corner consoles constrain the
  board only by the space they actually occupy.
* Mobile: landscape-only presentation, safe-area aware, with future touch-
  specific interaction assistance.
